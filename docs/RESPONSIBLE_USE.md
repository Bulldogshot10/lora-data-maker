# Responsible use

lora-data-maker is provided as an educational and research-oriented dataset
preparation project.

Please use it responsibly and respect the identity, privacy, consent, dignity,
and rights of people whose images or likenesses may appear in datasets.

Do not use this project to create or distribute datasets intended to impersonate,
harass, threaten, defame, exploit, or deceive real people without appropriate
consent and authorization. Do not assume that an image found online is free to
reuse, train on, or redistribute.

Users are responsible for the images, captions, tags, model inputs, and datasets
they collect or produce. Users must comply with applicable law, copyright and
licensing terms, privacy requirements, platform rules, and any permissions that
apply to their source material.

The project does not grant rights to third-party images, identities, likenesses,
trademarks, names, or source websites. A generated dataset may contain material
with separate ownership or licensing requirements.

The built-in scraper intentionally does not bypass CAPTCHA, login, or provider
verification. Respect source-site rules and access controls.

This document expresses the project's responsible-use policy and educational
intent. It does not modify or replace the Apache License 2.0 applicable to the
software.
